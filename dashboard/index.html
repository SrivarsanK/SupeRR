What I count as pressure. Three signals, read before each ball.

  • Required rate

    In a chase, 6 an over is routine and 15 is extreme.

  • Wickets lost

    Against the average for that over and innings.

  • Balls left

    The same trouble is harder to fix with four balls left.

The top 25% of balls on this score count as high pressure: 50,243 balls. Wickets fall 37% more often on them.
